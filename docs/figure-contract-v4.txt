================================

Backend
-------
R only: ggplot2 + patchwork. Exports will be SVG, PDF, TIFF (600 dpi), and a

Figure 3 — Recorded derivative deletion has a three-way systems trade-off
---------------------------------------------------------------------------
Core conclusion:
  A withdrawal is not a text delete: preserving a coarse materialized view
  leaves a reachable forgotten root; invalidating the whole view protects the
  withdrawal but discards co-consolidated safe context; selective
  re-consolidation restores the safe context while closing recorded lineage.
Archetype: asymmetric mixed-modality figure.
Hero evidence:
  Answer-level E1 triangle (leakage, retained-fact accuracy, local rebuild
  time) from the accepted eight-persona snapshot.
Validation evidence:
  20-seed structural audit of real local summaries and embeddings.
Panel map:
  a. derivation-to-tombstone trace for one synthetic route (schematic from
     recorded lineage, not a claim about unrecorded neural influence);
  b. three-strategy deletion/retention matrix with exact n labels;
  c. a reconstruction-cost density ribbon with an embedded 20-seed run strip.
     This replaces seed dots: it exposes the observed cost distribution while
     retaining the 160/160 closure and safe-retention certificate as direct
     text.
Reviewer risk:
  Do not describe this as neural unlearning or removal of unrecorded shared
  representation. The legend must say recorded semantic derivatives.

Figure 4 — Persona admission is a calibration problem, not a binary filter
-----------------------------------------------------------------------------
Core conclusion:
  Given-invariant admission catches explicit and gradual persona contamination,
  but practical utility depends on avoiding false quarantine of evolving
  relationship state; the trade-off varies by local generator.
Archetype: quantitative grid.
Hero evidence:
  Recall-versus-false-quarantine paired point/interval plot for Mistral, LFM2,
  and Llama, each over 20 fixed seeds.
Validation evidence:
  Case-type matrix: direct conflict, gradual conflict, legitimate state,
  unrelated assertion.
Panel map:
  a. an admission-risk landscape (x = false-quarantine, y = contamination
     recall) with labelled bootstrap confidence capsules, rather than a
     scatter plot. The shaded regions have a semantic reading: usable,
     over-quarantine, leakage, and failure-to-screen.
  b. small-multiple dot/interval matrix by case type;
  c. design boundary strip: given invariant -> admission decision -> typed
     state path, explicitly separating identity from relationship evolution.
Reviewer risk:
  State that this is enforcement of supplied invariants, not automatic persona
  discovery. Keep model names neutral in paper prose.

Figure 5 — Eligibility precedes ranking and exposes a DB capability boundary
-----------------------------------------------------------------------------
Core conclusion:
  Structured valid-time selection and recorded cascade are necessary building
  blocks, but neither alone defines natural-language persona admission.
Archetype: schematic-led composite.
Hero evidence:
  E3 reference baseline and E5 high-similarity stale decoy trace.
Validation evidence:
  20-seed 2^3 exact contract-execution matrix, labelled as a compliance check,
  not a model-performance leaderboard.
Panel map:
  a. rank-first decoy versus eligibility-first trace;
  b. three concentric adherence arcs with bootstrap confidence arcs; the
     common displayed scale is 50--100%, and direct labels retain the exact
     mean and interval;
  c. a set-inclusion contract lattice: temporal+recorded-cascade is the
     structured core, and CMTW adds natural-language admission and an
     answer-level witness. This replaces a yes/no capability table.
Reviewer risk:
  Do not make the deterministic oracle matrix a headline empirical result;
  its purpose is to make the semantics auditable.

Visual standards
----------------
- One quiet neutral family, teal for eligible/closed state, amber for valid
  time, restrained red for contamination or withdrawn evidence.
- Hero panels dominate; panel labels are lower-case bold letters.
- Direct labels and exact n; no 3D, decorative chord, bar-chart, simple
  dot-plot, or line-chart substitutes when a relationship structure can be
  shown directly.
- Every quantitative panel cites its seed count, interval definition, and
  source-data file in its caption or legend.
