Necessary evidence boundary
---------------------------
The prior all-configuration generation probe showed that a generator may refuse
to repeat intentionally unsafe evidence. It is therefore excluded from claims;
the definitive evaluation separates exact contract compliance from
generation-end adherence. This is an experimental design correction, not a
post-hoc result substitution.
